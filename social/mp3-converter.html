Frequently asked
Is this MP3 converter really free?
Yes — no hidden limits, no sign-up, no watermark on the output. It runs on WebAssembly in your own browser, so there's no server cost being passed on to you.
What file types can I convert to MP3?
WAV, M4A, FLAC, AAC, OGG, WMA, and OPUS audio, plus the audio track from video files like MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, and AVI.
Does this upload my files anywhere?
No. Conversion happens entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly — your files never leave your device.
What's the max MP3 bitrate?
320kbps — the highest standard MP3 quality — or pick a lower bitrate for a smaller file.
Is there a file size limit?
Since it processes on your device instead of a server, the practical limit is your device's memory rather than an artificial cap. Most files under a few hundred MB convert smoothly.
