Good to know
Does this upload my image anywhere?
No. Everything runs locally in your browser — the image is fetched directly from its source URL and never touches a server of ours.
Why does download sometimes fail?
Some sites block cross-origin downloads (CORS) for hotlinked images. When that happens, use "Open full size" and save the image directly from the new tab.
What image formats are supported?
JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, SVG, and most other formats your browser can render natively.
