About browser-kit

browser-kit is a free online platform that offers simple, fast, and useful tools to solve everyday digital problems. Whether it's counting words, removing whitespace, or using productivity tools, everything is designed to work instantly in your browser.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make powerful digital tools accessible to everyone — without requiring downloads, logins, or payments. We believe that useful tools should be simple, fast, and free.

What Makes browser-kit Different?

✔ No login required
✔ No data stored on servers
✔ 100% free forever
✔ Fast and lightweight tools
✔ Works globally on any device
